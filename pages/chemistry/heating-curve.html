The whole trick 关键就是选对公式:
Sloped line 斜线 temperature changes, no phase change → q = mcΔT
Flat plateau 平台 temperature constant, phase changes → q = mH
所以 heating curve 题必须分段计算，不能一个公式算到底。

Worked example · 分段计算 (m = 1 g)

Sloped segments for ice and steam use their own specific heat, which is not on Table B — the exam gives it if needed. 冰和蒸汽的比热不在 Table B 上，考试会另外给。

Segment length = heat absorbed, drawn to scale — notice how the boiling plateau (Hv=2260 J/g) is far longer than the melting plateau (Hf=334 J/g): vaporizing water takes much more energy than melting it. Specific heat of ice ≈ 2.11 and steam ≈ 2.01 J/(g·K) are typical textbook values (not on Table B). 段长 = 吸收的热量，按比例绘制。
