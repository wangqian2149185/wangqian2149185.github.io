The system, its state, and its boundary系统、状态，与它的边界

A system has a state: U, H, S, T, P, V… These are properties it owns. But heat (q) and work (w) are not owned — they are energy crossing the boundary. You can never say "this system contains 50 kJ of heat." Hover any symbol for its meaning and an analogy. 系统拥有一个状态：U、H、S、T、P、V……这些是它自身的属性。但热(q) 和功(w) 不被"拥有"——它们是穿过边界的能量。你永远不能说"这个系统里装着 50 kJ 的热"。把鼠标悬停在任意符号上，可看到含义与比喻。

Three conjugate pairs — the engines of change三对共轭变量——变化的引擎

Every way a system can change energy pairs an intensive "price" (how hard it pushes) with an extensive "amount" (how much moves). Energy change = price × amount moved.系统改变能量的每一种方式，都把一个强度量"价格"（推得多用力）和一个广延量"数量"（移动了多少）配成一对。能量变化 = 价格 × 移动的数量。

Which potential should I watch?我该盯着哪个势函数？

Pick your experimental conditions. The right potential is the one that is minimized when the system settles — its sign tells you the direction of spontaneous change.选择你的实验条件。正确的势函数，就是系统安定下来时被"最小化"的那个——它的符号告诉你自发变化的方向。

Spontaneity checker · ΔG = ΔH − TΔS自发性判断器 · ΔG = ΔH − TΔS

At constant T and P, a process runs forward on its own when ΔG < 0. Enthalpy (ΔH) and entropy (ΔS) pull against each other, and temperature decides who wins.在恒温恒压下，当 ΔG < 0 时过程自发正向进行。焓 (ΔH) 与熵 (ΔS) 相互拉扯，而温度决定谁占上风。
